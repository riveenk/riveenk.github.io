<p>A dark void appears from the lengths<br />Of my sight: An uncommon sight, both to man<br />And to the dead. Death.<br />And from, a frightened pair of gleaming eyes<br />Lies affixed on my bloody hands, horrified;<br />Her scythe clutched, twined with her fingers<br />While my delicate tools loosen free from mine.</p>
<p>Then I take a step back, another, and another,<br />So she could check what soul she could salvage<br />From my beautiful art of blood, meat and bones<br />To see if she could best my artwork with her<br />Expertise; yet I see her hands twitch and tremble<br />Still in fear and agitation. I'm Surprised.</p>
<p>I then think of moments earlier, as I was carving<br />Into the body of my friend, remaking, repainting <br />The works of God inside with my scalpel. Euphoric.<br />Even then I knew who was lurking outside my<br />Peripheral; She was too pure a void to miss.</p>
<p>Now, she stands up and looks to her side as if<br />My model is with her... My own artwork.<br />She pauses, then tears stream from her eyes <br />Into the darkness of the cloak<br />And then she bursts into tears. Sad. Angry. Afraid.</p>
<p>And I smiled, and I laughed.</p>
