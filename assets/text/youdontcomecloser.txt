<p>Frosty hints in the ruthless air,<br /> No nights like this ever turned fair.<br /> The trees are bare&mdash;it's almost December.<br /> This eve will be one to remember.</p>
<p>You don't come closer, or hold my hand.<br /> You pretend you don't notice a strand<br /> begins to run down my face,<br /> or sense that my heart has begun to race.</p>
<p>The coffee is cold, just like my mother.<br /> It burns my lips when I beg it to smother<br /> my racing heart with too much caffeine.<br /> Yet fails to warm when the frost is mean.</p>
<p>The food is absent, just like my father.<br /> It will arrive when the hunger doesn't bother<br /> anymore and I'm half full with my cold coffee.<br /> I can barely have anything bigger than a toffee.</p>
<p>You don't come closer, or flinch too hard.<br /> But you tap your fingers like you'd guard<br /> my scarred heart until it all healed<br /> and no more of raw wounds stay revealed.</p>
<p>Am I bothering you? Is this too much?<br /> I'd hate myself to use you as a crutch.<br /> I've never told this much about me<br /> to anyone, because there's always a fee.</p>
<p>You don't come closer, or tap your feet anymore.<br /> You look down like you'd scrape the whole floor<br /> just to collect the broken pieces of my soul<br /> and glue it all together to make me whole.</p>
<p>The food arrives, a warm bowl of mac and cheese.<br /> But I can't eat more and keep my belly at ease.<br /> You can have it, maybe this is your fee.<br /> I think I'll have a nice cup of tea.</p>
<p>I dread going home&mdash;it's almost December.<br /> But this night has been one to remember.<br /> I'll let you watch me finish my tea,<br /> and secretly hope you, too, hate me.</p>
