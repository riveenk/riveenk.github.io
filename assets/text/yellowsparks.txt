<p>In the loving cradle of relative safety <br /> I tell the children of a tale down south. <br /> They paint the skies with yellow sparks <br /> And cheer all night with smoke around.</p>
<p>They have no children, no sick, no elderly&mdash; <br /> They're only others, the cost of this art. <br /> When they are gone, we&rsquo;ll build a new home <br /> And kick some dirt over the criminal's bones.</p>
<p>&ldquo;Why?&rdquo; they question, &ldquo;why the yellow sparks? <br /> We have no sparks, and we love them too.&rdquo; <br /> You are children. You are loved. <br /> They are targets that must be crushed.</p>
<p>Yet they pester, with minds corrupt&mdash; <br /> &ldquo;They are human too,&rdquo; they say. <br /> &ldquo;They would love to play with us <br /> And paint our skies with yellow sparks.&rdquo;</p>
<p>They know not of the others&rsquo; horror, <br /> They speak in tongues foreign to us. <br /> This home is ours, and they, a tenant; <br /> The rent is due&mdash;and the price is death.</p>
