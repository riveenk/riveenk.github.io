<p>From a distant frame pulling away from you<br /> I see the flicker of your dancing figures<br /> eclipsing against the dark starless sky,<br /> as the growing minutes fill the space between us.</p>

<p>Each spin in your dance, his hand in yours,<br /> tugs at the strings in the depths of my heart.<br /> It's not love, or a magical charm.<br /> It's the force of my heart breaking apart.</p>

<p>At nights when I think of you, I dream of a<br /> system with just the two of us. Just our hands,<br /> our gravity, and love, holding us together as<br /> we spin about our common focal point.</p>

<p>But the cold chaos of the near-empty space<br /> torments me with threats from corners afar.<br /> He waltzes in to exchange hands, steal my dance,<br /> and banish me from my own system.</p>

<p>My surface still bears traces of your face,<br /> the light of your shine felt right in my eyes.<br /> But your flickering lights fade away from my eyes<br /> and your pull on my heart slips away from me.</p>

<p>I still think of you every now and then,<br /> but you are too busy holding his hands.<br /> You no longer feel the shades of my gravity<br /> and I barely feel the pull from your heart.</p>
