<p>Spring-touched breeze and a soothing sun&mdash;<br /> the days were bright, but the nights were young.<br /> Candles lit, their sins were sung.<br /> The Asian Spring has just begun.</p>

<p>The wind will sing in freedom's voice<br /> as we march in peaceful stride.<br /> But alas! Our hearts have sighed,<br /> for the wretched ones neglect our choice</p>

<p>sitting on their thrones of bones&mdash;yours and mine,<br /> in their palaces&mdash;bound together in tears and blood,<br /> lying drunk and dancing, as our sorrows flood,<br /> in joyous tones, the damned lot, in old exotic wine.</p>

<p>Spineless fools sold their souls for a measly price<br /> and forgot who fights for them. They think the foes<br /> hold banners in hand, and concern in heart, but the world knows<br /> the red scarfs have bargained the fools' pride for a pint and rice.</p>

<p>Blood was spilled, ours and theirs, both undeserved.<br /> Frightened eyes watched khaki figures fight<br /> their own kin, as the burning buses leaked cruel light.<br /> Then night fell, hearts burning, yet too many unnerved.</p>

<p>We stand together as children of one<br /> and watch the wretched Mynah fly.<br /> Dance, my friends, better days draw nigh.<br /> Under the soothing sun, rebirth has begun.</p>
